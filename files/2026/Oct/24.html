Saturday 24th October

We managed to get to the High Street and back just before it started to rain. Then it rained for the rest of the day and into the night.

After doing the weekly accounts I went into the garage to try to fit the final bolt to fix the seat to the floor. After loosening the other bolts (apart from the offside rear) and hammering a wooden wedge under the catch to give space to get the bolt in, I managed to find the hole and screw in the bolt. Then I put up the hood.

After lunch we spoke to Victoria. Yesterday, Brandon was offered the job in West Chicargo. He will try to 'negotiate' on salary and relocation expenses. They now need to work out all the details!

Victoria had been to talk to the children's teachers. It seems that both are doing very well.
