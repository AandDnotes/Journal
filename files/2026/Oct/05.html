Monday 5th October

Once again the morning started misty, which cleared later in the morning to become a sunny, warm and calm day. While Angela went to her Monday Club, I completed the monthly accounts and then updated the report of the RCEA's last talk with comments by Mike W.

Having signed-up yesterday to the Eurotherm Retirees Christmas lunch, I sent my form today for the RCEA Christmas lunch, also paying for both of us. It looks as though I will have four Christmas lunches, the Lunch Club and the Sussex Wanderers being the others.

I gave the garage door a final coat of wood stain and finally got my bitbot to go forwards and backwards and steer. I can now 'play' with lights and sound.
