Wednesday 7th October

It was wet when we got up and continued until later in the afternoon. So Angela and her walking friends got wet. I spent a lot of the morning trying to get the printer working. I had received the route instructions for next Wednesday's Wanderers run and decided to print them. But the printer was marked as in error. I replaced all the Epson software, but eventually had to delete the printer and then add it.

So I printed the route instructions and then printed a speaker's certificate for next week's IET talk.

After lunch I 'played' with the BitBot, adding some LED illumination according to the forward/reverse and left/right status. I decided against adding sound, since it would be annoying.

After dinner I went to the Steyning Centre to see this month's film, called 'Tuner'. It was very good and quite well attended.
