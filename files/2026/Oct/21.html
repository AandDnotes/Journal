Wednesday 21st October

This morning the rain was horizontal at times. Angela got very wet on her walk. I deleted a few more files and then started producing a web page recording the work that Lee did on the B. Lee sent the paint code.

Angela got home just before I went off in the B to the WSMGOC pub run. Luckily it had stopped raining and there were even some sunny spells. I drove to the Roundabout Hotel, where I joined the others for a carvery. My B was the only MG there! I got home at about three o'clock.

I have a problem with the driver's seat. The catch has lost it's spring and so the seat moves as I press the clutch pedal to the floor. This makes the gear change difficult.

Later I sent Lee's details to Colin N for the WSMGOC recommended garages and also sent his details to the MGOC.
