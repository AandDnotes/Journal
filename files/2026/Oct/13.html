Tuesday 13th October

This morning I downloaded the attendance list for the evening's IET talk and produced the printed list.

After an early lunch (dinner) I picked up Peter W and went to Field Place for the RCEA talk on Tram Trains. It was interesting, but I didn't get home until nearly five. Then I left before 5:30 to go to Brighton University for an IET Sussex talk. The traffic at Hollingbury on the A27 and then on Lewes Road took an hour to do half hour journey. I just had time to set up the camera and microphones. Mike U was late, so I introduced the speaker, Tom. He did a good job.

I got home at nine o'clock.
