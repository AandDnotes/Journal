Thursday 15th October

At the start of the week we had expected that today we would pack our cases and take the train to Gatwick this afternoon. However, it turned into an ordinary Thursday after our cruise on the Danube was cancelled.

I found that my video upload on YouTube had put it on to my channel instead of the IET Sussex channel! Eventually I found that, although I was viewing the Sussex channel, I was logged into my channel and so had to change it. So eventually the video of September's talk got there.

So we started our 'usual' Thursday by going to Lyons Farm to do the weekly shop. The Bostal was closed, so we had to go the long way around. Naturally we came home via Findon Vale.

We couldn't get the garage door 'zapper' to open the door. Later, when I checked the voltage of the relatively new batteries, they had dropped from 3 volts to 2.5 volts. So I ordered a new device from Amazon.

After lunch I retrieved the video files from the camera for Tuesday's talk and reduced their size using Handbrake. I also checked that the refund from Riviere Travel had arrived in the bank and moved some money into the savings account.
