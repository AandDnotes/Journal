Saturday 3rd October

Although we had our usual start for a Saturday by walking to the High Street, we started by going to the Penfold Hall to look at the art exhibition. There were a lot of good paintings, all by local amature artists.

We then went to the Farmers Market to buy potatoes and apples. I also bought a bottle of Surrey white wine. Shopping at the market is more of an event. We then bought a newspaper and some rolls.

We didn't speak to Victoria since she asked if we could talk tomorrow.

Otherwards, I did the weekly account and started updating the monthly accounts.
