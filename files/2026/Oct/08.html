Thursday 8th October

We set off to Lyons Farm to do our weekly grocery shop, going into both Lidl and Sainsbury's. We also filled the Panda with very expensive petrol.

On our way home we called into Findon Vale garden centre, where I bought a pair of shoes. They don't have a thin, flexible sole, but they are reasonably narrow.

Later I investigated the down-pipe on the garage. We had wondered for a long time why the slab under water butt was often wet. Angela noticed a drip of water from the water diverter, so I took the top off the diverter to find the down-pipe full of water!. I removed the diverter (and a piece of down-pipe) and then removed the old diverter, but the water had gone. So I put it back together.
