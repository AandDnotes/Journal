Thursday 29th October

A grey, overcast, cool, but calm day. We started by going to Lidl and Sainsbury's at Lyons Farm to do the weekly shop. Then we went on to Findon Vale, where, after coffee, Angela bought two bags of compost, four cyclamen and 18 pansies. These are for the hanging baskets and troughs.

The rubber bushes for the rocker box cover that I ordered yesterday arrived today. So I fitted them to the B. I found that the old bushes were hard and compressed. I added an entry to my 'Small Jobs' web page.

While I was doing that, Angela went to the High Street to have her hair cut. The hairdresser was running late, so we had a late dinner.
