Sunday 18th October

The day started sunny, but cold. Later it clouded over and there was a shower. Angela went to church and stayed for a meeting and lunch.

I rewired the off-side brake light to make it work, raised the roof and investigated the driver's seat runners. There is no spring on the 'clip', but it now looks easier to remove the seat.

Google told me that I had exceeded my allocation, so I spent quite some time deleting email and files. A lot of space is used by videos in Google Photos, most of which were uploaded by others. Brandon has set up an application on his server and copied all the Google Photos photos to it. We might be able to use it for future sharing.
