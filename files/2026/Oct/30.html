Friday 30th October

This morning we set off in the mist to Shoreham, where we caught the 700 bus to Hove. The idea was to find out how to resurrect Angela's iPhone 'pay as you go' SIM, from '3'. Our first port of call, a Vodafone shop in Hove, said they didn't have access to the full'3' system and directed us to a '3' shop opposite Churchill Square. So it was back on the bus. It seems that, because we hadn't used the phone for six months, the SIM had been cancelled. We tried the other main phone networks, but none now provide this type of 'classic pay as you go' SIM.

Later I applied for a 'gifgaf' SIM, although Angela did not receive any email, so we don't know if it will arrive tomorrow as stated.

While in Brighton we had a look around the relatively new IKEA in Churchill Square, but it took us a long time to find our way out! Then we had an early lunch in M&S before catching the bus back to Shoreham.

I had a further 'play' with the RCEA report, but still failed to get some of the images to move to right side of the panel.
