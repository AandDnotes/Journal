Friday 2nd October

We left quite early to get to my appointment at the skin clinic at ten o'clock. There were no traffic queues, so we were half an hour early. Angela walked down to Morrisons to get some cash and couple of items. I was only there for a few minutes and I was called in. It seems that they had two appointments for 10am and were hoping that someone would arrive early. I was done by ten to ten and had to wait for Angela to get back.

The nurse removed the (self dissolving stitches from both near my ear on on my nose. The former was quite painful. She seemed to think that both areas looked good and I now just need to put a little Vasaline on each area daily.

From Worthing we drove back through Steyning and on to Ashurst to go to the St Barnabas coffee morning. We didn't know many people there. However, it seems that we won a raffle prize, a Christmas decoration!

During the afternoon the window man called to replace the broken window in the dining room.
