Thursday 22nd October

The weather forecasts are dominated by two approaching storms, but today was sunny practically all day. So our trip to Lyons Farm and then on to Findon Vale took place in the sunshine.

I tried to buy a battery from the B's clock. But, Sainsbury's, B&Q and Currys didn't have one. When I got home I ordered a pack of ten for £3 with free delivery!

I had a call from Martin G, who was at the MGOC Workshop, asking if I wanted anything from the spares shop. I said that I am missing a seat adjuster catch spring He phoned later to say that the workshop had found one, probably from the bin! I found that the spring was difficult to reach, so I removed the seat, which would also allow me to try to make is slide more easily.

I spent some time cleaning and trying to straighten the runners. Then Martin phoned again and called round at about 5:30, with the spring and a copy of the MGOC's 'white book'.
