Wednesday 28th October

While Angela went walking in the sunshine with her friends, I put the registration link into my Adestra email and submitted it for approval. Unfortunately it wasn't, because I'd asked for it to be sent out next week, when the SE regional email will be sent out. So I re-submitted it for immediate sending.

Before lunch, having removed the spark plugs from the B, I adjusted the tappet clearance. I had to turn the engine using the fan, which was quite tricky. The tappers were not very well adjusted. After lunch I put the rocker box cover back on. I found that the rubber seals have gone hard, so I ordered some new ones from the MGOC. The postage was about four times the cost of the two seals.

I spent some time working on the RCEA report on the last talk. I completed it apart from a couple of problems with image location.

Later I ordered a torch that plugs into the cigarette lighter socket. It also has a USB socket for charging devices, so we could use it to charge things from the car batteries if we lose power.
