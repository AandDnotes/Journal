Saturday 17th October

A chilly, but very sunny day, and quite a busy one. We started with our usual walk to the High Street to buy a newspaper and some rolls. Then we drove to the other side of Lewis to pick up the B. Lee explained what he had done underneath and in the sills and later sent some photos of the underside.

We stopped at Daisy's Tearoom for coffee and were home just before lunch. Angela noticed that the offside brake light was not working. The bulb was fine, but it looks as though Lee has plugged the brake light bulb into the wrong connector. I'll try moving it tomorrow.

I fitted Austin/Juniper/Brandon's seat belt holders. They seem to work well, although the driver's side catches on the roof frame. However, I can maneuver the frame around it.

While I was 'playing' with the B, Angela cut the grass.
