Friday 23rd October

I had two main objectives today; make the B's driver's seat slide easily and refit the seat in the car. I managed the first, but have not yet completed the second.

At first I concentrated on the part of the slide that is fixed to the floor, but then I discovered that the runner on one side of the seat was bent. This was due to the tubular steel frame of the seat having been bent. I tried to straighten it, but ended up putting two washers between the steel tube and the runner. I also fitted the second-hand spring to the catch.

Refitting the seat was more of a problem. I inserted the rear bolts firs, as suggested by John Twist of Universal Motors in one of his videos. I then slid the seat as far back as it goes and fitted the middle bolt, although finding the hole took a while. When I first looked at removing the seat I found that the catch was over the bolt. Now, having installed the spring on the catch, it was again over the bolt! I bent the catch and tried levering it up, but gave up as dinner time approached.

After lunch I had a short Teams call with Dave P and Norman W. We discovered that Norman had been given access to the wrong application!
