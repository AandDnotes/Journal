Saturday 31st October

After a misty start, the sun soon came out and, with no wind, it felt warm in the sunshine. After our walk to the High Street to buy a newspaper I checked the timing on the B. When I lowered the hood it broke the offside seatbelt guide! Then, in order to rotate the distributor I had to not only loosen the pinch bolt, but also loosen one of the clamp bolts (after I removed the dip stick). I found that the existing position gave the fastest idle speed. Then I took the car for a test drive and bought some petrol.

After lunch we spoke with Victoria. The company that has offered Brandon a job agreed to a higher salary, more relocation expenses and the later starting date. He is now trying to find a short-lease apartment so that they can see out the school year. They are also thinking of buying a house without needing to sell their existing house (with a mortgage).
