Wednesday 25th November

While Angela went walking with her friends I replied to an email or two and then finished my article on 'choices' for Main Gear. Afterwards I updated a couple of pages on my MGB website.

After lunch, while Angela went to the library, I went into the garage. Firstly I put a second coat of paint on the two 'chips' and put some clear lacquer on the patch under the bonnet. I also took the cover off the battery bins an measured the space between the clamp bolts. As it happens, the potential battery has its shortest dimension under the clamp. In the process I broke the seatbelt guide (again). So I cut two lengths of nail to help strengthen the joint. I then glued it back together.

I added a couple of notes on the CC-UK draft document on hybrid events.
