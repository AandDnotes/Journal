Friday 6th November

With a little help from Angela I spent most of the morning and some of the afternoon cleaning the study. I removed everything on the ground and then worked from the top down, mainly sucking up dust. In the process I found the little spring from the torch (under the filing cabinet).

I tried once more to remove the end cap on the torch, but no joy. So I put it back in the packaging (turning the outside one inside out) and took it down to the Post Office. All I had to do was let the person behind the counter scan the QR code that I'd retrieved from the AliExpress web page. She printed a label and stuck it on the package. Job done!

While I was doing all this, Angela planted three new hanging baskets and the 'manger' on the walled garden wall. All in all we are tired out.
