Friday 27th November

Today was really about the WSMGOC pub run to Cootham. Earlier in the morning I checked the B's battery and found that it was 95% charged. But the engine took a lot of cranking before it fired and took a few more revolutions before it caught.

We went off about 11:30, arriving at The Crown just after midday. Most people were already there, but, including ours, there were only four MGs; another B (Ian's 1973 green B with wire wheels), Ken's Magnette and a ZR (based on a Rover 200). It was a little drizzly on the way there and then rained for the rest of the day.

We had a good meal, followed by a desert and coffee. There must have been about twenty club members and we got home around three o'clock.

When we got home I noticed that the front side lights were not working. Earlier I'd also noticed that the ignition warning light was not on when I'd turned the key, but flashed before the engine started! S0 I spent some time looking at wiring diagrams and the Haynes manual, about the these two problems and the poor starting.
