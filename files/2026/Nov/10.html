Tuesday 10th November

A tiring afternoon. Angela took the bus to Storrington during the morning to bank cash from the Lunch Club. I put together the attendance list for the evening's IMechE/IET talk.

After lunch I was picked up by Peter W and taken to Field Place for a talk by Bernard Challen. When I got home I left soon after to drive to Crawley College for the IMechE/IET talk. We had a good audience and the presentation went down well. It's a pity that it was raining and to quite a tiring journey.
