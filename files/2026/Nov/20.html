Friday 20th November

We were up early this morning. Angela walked down to the church to help Phyllis set up her stall for tomorrow's Bazaar. I was picked up by Mike W and driven to Rustington for the RCEA committee meeting. We started half an hour earlier than usual because of a booking class.

I had a reply from the MSK unit, saying that I'm on their list for a routine MSK Hip appointment. So I bought some travel insurance from AllClear, just saying that I had Arrythmia, and then phoned Saga to tell them that I had arranged insurance for our river cruise in April.

Later in the afternoon I went to the Hygenist (£75 to be tortured), while Angela continued cooking for the Bazaar. I also added a little to my article on choices.
