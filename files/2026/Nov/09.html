Monday 9th November

Angela went off early to help with the Lunch Club. Whereas I soldered plug/sockets on to the battery charger and the crocodile clips. I then fitted the 'charger' cable to the B's battery. I then charged the battery from 42% to 100%. Later in the morning I went to the Lunch Club.

We were home by 2:30 and so had a quiet afternoon.

Later in the afternoon (after dark), I took the B to the WSMGOC meeting at Washington. I won a raffle prize and chose a bottle of white wine. When I got home, the battery was at 73%.
