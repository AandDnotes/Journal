Wednesday 18th November

A busy, but at times frustrating day. While Angela went off walking with her friends, I took my photo and applied for a new passport. Luckily, this time, my photo was accepted by the online system. I had to send back the old passport, so I put it in an envelope and took it to the Post Office. Unfortunately I'd folded the envelope around the passport, so there was not enough space to stick on the bar code, etc. So the lady in the Post Office removed the tape and unfolded the envelope. However, it cost over £10 to send it.

Since the weather was fine I hung out the washing and then went into the garage to fit the new windscreen wiper arms and blades. The old arms took some getting off. Counter-intuitively I had to lever the arm on the opposite side to the spring clip in order to gradually lift it off. I cleaned the splines on the drive and used a little copper grease on them to help get the new arms on. However, I'm not sure they are fully on, even after tapping them with a rubber hammer. Hopefully they will not work their way off. At least they now have a good sweep.

The frustration was due to trying to log into the CC-UK SharePoint. It rejected the password (and the possible other ones). So I reset it, but the verification via MS Authenticator would not work. I had to use a code via an SMS. All I wanted to do was upload my document on 'collaboration'.

Finally I made a small edit to the RCEA report, pointed out by Glyn, and made it public.
