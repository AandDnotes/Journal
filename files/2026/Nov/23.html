Monday 23rd November

A cold day. I don't think the temperature got above five degrees. Angela went off to her Monday Club and I produced and submitted this month's Adestra email.

I then spent some time trying to get iMovie to access the library. I eventually found the problem and so put together the video of the last IET talk. I had to extract some bits of the audio track from the microphone recordings. I exported the file and ran it through Handbrake to reduce its size. It is now ready to upload to YouTube.

In spite of the cold, I went into the garage to inspect the small patch of paint. Whereas it was light when wet, it was dark when dry! I put another coat on it. I also checked the battery, which was shown as being 100% charged.
