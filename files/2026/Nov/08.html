Sunday 8th November

A very misty start to the day and, even when the mist cleared and the sun came out, the temperature didn't get above seven degrees. Angela went off to church and I prepared things for Tuesday's IET talk. I created the speaker's certificate and the start slide. I also emailed Norman and prepared the attendance list, ready for the final list.

Later I went into the garage to start the B. From my battery monitor I found that the battery was only 78% charged! The car only just started, but then charged the battery up to 45%. So I brought the charger and the connectors inside and prepared them for soldering. The idea is that I can plug the charger into the battery without removing the cover, but still connect the original crocodile clips to the charger.

Later still, we spoke with Paul. They had been to look at a thatched farmhouse that is for sale nearby. It looks very interesting.
