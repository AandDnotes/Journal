Saturday 14th November

An even harder frost this morning, with only a little sunshine. Angela was collected at ten past nine and taken to Eastbourne for a church meeting. She got home about four o'clock. I spent the morning doing the weekly accounts, including reconciling my spreadsheet against the bank statement.

After lunch I went to the Repair Cafe, which was quite busy. Afterwards a lot of us went to the Rising Sun, since Robin was buying (and it was the last cafe for this year).

When I got home we spoke to Victoria and then had a late dinner.
