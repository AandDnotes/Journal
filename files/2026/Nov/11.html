Wednesday 11th November

Armistice Day, and a damp one. There was drizzle for most of the morning. Angela went off walking and Martin G arrived at about 9:45. It was the Sussex Wanderers run, but my usual partner, David F-B, had double booked, so couldn't come. Also, Martin's 'navigator' was not available. Since it was raining he was not keen to take his MGB, so I took mine.

I drove up to Warnham, where the Wanderers met at the Sussex Oak. After coffee and a natter (and two minutes silence) we set off. Although we had the route instructions, we were following two other MGs, an RV8 and a Chinese MG. The RV8 missed a turn and both following cars blindly followed. However, they soon got back on route, but towards the end they pulled into a petrol station (and we both followed). So Martin and I continued on our own, finishing the defined route. Overall it was a good run and a good lunch (a chicken curry).

In total I drove the B 29 miles, which is the furthest I've driven the car in one day. Although I'd put Rainex on the windscreen, the wipers didn't clear the whole sweep and didn't clear the raindrops. So I need to get new blades.
