Monday 27th July

This morning we were up at 7:30 so as to have breakfast and then an early lunch. Apart from drinking coffee I'm not sure what we did during the rest of the morning apart from taking a 'group' photo.

However, we ate early and then seven of us drove off to the Water Bus stop. We caught the Water Bus to Dordreckt, at the end of the 'line'. We then walked around the city, had ice cream, walked some more, had coffee by the river and then caught the bus back. It was a good day and the afternoon was warm, breezy and with plenty of sunshine.
