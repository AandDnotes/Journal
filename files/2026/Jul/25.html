Saturday 25th July

We had a day off to recover from yesterday's exertions. My thigh muscle kept me awake, so I had a short nap before lunch.

Although the morning was overcast it was warm and the afternoon became sunny. So some of us sat outside for a while and played an interesting game based around the Tokyo metro system. After dinner some played a new game (which was very complicated) and some of us watched 'Death on the Nile'. It was a pity that the noise from the game made it difficult to hear the TV and the sub-titles were quite quick to read. But it was a good film.
