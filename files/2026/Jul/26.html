Sunday 26th July

It was eight o'clock before we got up. We then had a quiet day because it was overcast and rained during the early afternoon. Then some took Hilda for a walk, but I didn't go out.

During the afternoon the children (with Paul) went to the cottage to play a game and the rest of us played Wingspan. It took about four hours! Later we planned our outing to Dordrecht on the water bus tomorrow. Rowan has a tennis lesson, so she and Krista will not go.
