Friday 24th July

This morning we set off fairly early (well, about ten thirty) and drove to Zeist, to the south-east of Utrecht. The journey took about an hour and a half, but then we had a five kilometer walk through the forest. We then drove to another forest area, but to a cafe that served pancakes. The cafe was large and very popular and the pancakes were good. We then did a very short walk to an old fort that happened to be closed. So we drove home (another hour and a half). All in all a good day out, in spite of the journeys.

A good part of the evening was spent placing an order for food.
