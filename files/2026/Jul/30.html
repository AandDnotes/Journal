Thursday 30th July

Another warm, sunny and breezy day, with still no sign of rain in the forecast. We started by doing some grocery shopping at Lyons Farm, calling into Boots to print some photos and to buy a new beard trimmer. We came straight home since we were expecting someone to call to service our boiler from midday onwards. He didn't actually arrive until about 2:15 and found that our boiler was in excellent condition, requiring no adjustment.

I spent some time drafting the next RCEA newsletter, using the new web site. I ran into a couple of problems, so emailed Steve, the web man.
