Saturday 26th September

Saturday morning normally consists of a walk to the High Street to buy a newspaper (and perhaps a few extra things), followed by updating the weekly accounts. Today was no exception.

After lunch I messaged Victoria, who had got up late and wanted another half hour. But her half hour is usually a bit longer and on this occasion it was about an hour. Brandon thought his trip to West Chicago had gone well. The company had extended the interview for the whole day.

I had a chance to test my code for the bit:bot. It didn't work, but I think I know why.
