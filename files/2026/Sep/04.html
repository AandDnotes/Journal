Friday 4th September

As expected, we were down first for breakfast at 8:30. K & D arrived at 9:15. So we stayed in the breakfast room until they had finished.

Kate then took us off to Long Melford, firstly just to have a look up and down the main road and then into Melford Hall, a National Trust property, that, once in, we realised that that we had been there before. When I looked back at our visits to Suffolk on our web pages I found that we had visited Melford Hall in 2024 when we met at Sudbury. Nevertheless, we had a god introductory talk from one of the volunteer guides and then looked around the house before having lunch in their cafe.

We then walked up the hill to the very large parish church and had a quick look around inside before returning to Greyhound Cottage.

After showing each other photos of our families, Kate took us back into Long Melford to the 'Cock and Bell' pub for dinner. THe atmosphere was no where as good as the pub we visited the night before, but the food was reasonable. We got back to Greyhound Cottage a little before 9:30.
