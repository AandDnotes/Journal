Thursday 3rd September

We left home at 10:30 heading for Glemsford in Suffolk to meet up with Kate and Dick. After a stop for coffee at Clacketts Lane and a lunch stop at Hatfield Forest (missing the correct turn off the M11 yet again), we arrived at 4:30. Kate and Dick were already there. Greyhound Cottage is quite something, parts of it dating back to the thirteenth century. It was a tiring, but uneventful journey.

After a cup of tea with K & D and then a glass or two of bubbly, we walked to the pub in the village for dinner. It was quite busy (and noisy), but we had a good meal.

As Angela said, Dick is looking his age. He has more of a stoop than me (bending from the waist), walks with two sticks and then has to stop to catch his breath. He has been using an electric buggy for some time.
