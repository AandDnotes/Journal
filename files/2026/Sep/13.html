Sunday 13th September

While Angela went to church I looked at the toaster that I'd brought home from the Repair Cafe. I discovered that it had five elements, rather than the six drawn on a sketch by the owner, and all slots got hot. After removing the base and some of the links between elements I could only find one that had broken, rather than four as diagnosed by the owner. So I replaced it with one of the new elements provided by the owner.

I messaged Robin to ask who has the PAT tester and phoned the owner to give an update. Then I unpacked my tool bag.

During the afternoon we had a fairly short talk with Paul. Greet and Wim had delivered Albert (the tortoise) to them to look after while they are away.

Before lunch Angela had walked back down to the church to take tomorrow's main coarse out of the freeze to thaw. Before starting to cook our dinner we walked down there to put the dishes in the fridge.
