Saturday 12th September

We made our usual trip to the High Street this morning to buy a newspaper and some food items. Then I did the weekly accounts and Angela pottered in the garden.

After lunch I went to the Repair Cafe. I checked the wiring of someones very old standard lamp and advised on how to fit a plug. Then I fitted a new in-line switch to someones table lamp. Unfortunately I concluded that the motor was faulty on their desk fan. I also brought home a large toaster, together with a number of new elements. We were quite busy, with only Andy and I on the electrical bench.

When I got home we had a call with Victoria.
