Tuesday 8th September

It rained during the night and then continued to rain until after two o'clock, sometimes quite heavily. Then the sun came out, although it was never very warm. Unfortunately Angela decided to walk to the High Street this morning and got very wet.

I brought our accounts up to date after all our spending while away. Then I set up a template for 'trips' web pages, making them responsive, but looking like the others. I started putting together a page for our trip to Suffolk.
