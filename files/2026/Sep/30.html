Wednesday 30th September

We were a little surprised to see it raining this morning. Angela was driven off in Felicity's car to meet up with the rest of their walking friends. However, the rain stopped and Angela was hot in her waterproof over-trousers.

I had mailed a problem report to Steve the website man, but this morning found an extra check box that solved the problem with the images! So I tidied up the visit report and sent Mike W and Glyn M a link so that they could review it. Then I set about drafting a report on this month's talk. I spent a long time adding shadows to images, only to find that they looked odd on panels with a grey background. So I replaced them all with flat images.

After lunch I brushed down the garage door and gave it a coat of stain. Both bottom corners of the door are rotten, so I don't know how much longer it will last.
