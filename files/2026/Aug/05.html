Wednesday 5th August

Our 54th wedding anniversary. Angela went walking with her friends and I finished (almost) my write-up of our trip to Berkenwoude.

After an early lunch we went into Worthing to a skin clinic for someone to look at the patch on my nose. He said that they will arrange another appointment so that they can cut it out! It may require a skin graft.

From there we stopped at Waitrose for a coffee and then set off to Chichester. After a walk around part of the city we had tea in the Cathedral cafe and then walked to the theatre, where we sat outside before going in for coffee and cake.

Finally we saw their production of 'My Fair Lady', which was very good. However, it finished quite late and the drive home was not very pleasant. However, before we went in to the auditorium we did hear that Victoria et al had got home, after spending a night in a hotel, presumably in Minniapolis.
