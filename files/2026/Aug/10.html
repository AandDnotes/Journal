Monday 10th August

Today we were officially classed as being in a drought. It wasn't too hot, but mainly sunny and no hint of rain. This morning Angela met her Monday Club friends at someone's house. I think they are meeting here next week, so I will have to keep out of the way.

Before it got too hot I cut the bush on the corner of our drive with the electric hedge cutter. It looks a lot better, but is probably still too big to see over when reversing the car off the drive. Otherwise, I updated my 'pandemics' web page within my 'Reminiscences of an Ordinary Man' site. Plus reading the newspaper.
