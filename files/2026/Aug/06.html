Thursday 6th August

After getting up a little later than usual we went off to Lyons Farm to do the grocery shop. Then off to Findon Vale for coffee!

After lunch I pruned the apple tree. At least, I shortened the spurs, taking them back to where they were cut last year (following Monty Don's advice). I also tied in the horizontal growth.

Then I booked a hotel room at Gatwick prior to our flight to Budapest in October. I also booked train tickets there and back.
