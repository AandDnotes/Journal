Monday 17th August

This morning Angela's Monday Club bet at our house. So I locked myself away in the study. I sent a letter to the Editor of the Telegraph about the lack of relevant experience of the managers of DESNZ. It took me three attempts so as to send in the right form and with the necessary information. Then spent some time completing my 'Downsizing' page on my 'Reminiscences of an Ordinary Man' web site.

We went to the WSMGOC meeting at Washington. We both had 'half plate' meals. I won the last-but-one raffle prize, a plastic mug with an MG logo. We were home at nine.
