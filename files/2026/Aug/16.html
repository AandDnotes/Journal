Sunday 16th August

Definitely cooler today, although still up to 25 °C and so feeling quite warm.

While Angela went off to church I spent some time lubricating all th window and door hinges. It just seemed as though it was time to do it. We then talked with Paul (and Hilda) during the afternoon. Their week had been as exciting as ours.

After dinner, having read an article in the newspaper, I drafted a 'Letter to the Editor' about the suitability of the ministers and management of the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero.
