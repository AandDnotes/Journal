Saturday 29th August

It rained overnight and for most of the morning, stopping for a while just before we got home from our walk to the High Street. The rained returned for a while during the afternoon.

I did the weekly accounts and added a little to my 'political philosophy' notes. Also, the BitBot arrived back. Although the man at 4tronix said he had replaced components, but the device looked like a new one.

After lunch we spoke with Victoria. Brandon has had one online interview and overall seems quite hopeful. The end seems to be in sight for their house painting and they've had their pool emptied and measured for a new liner.
