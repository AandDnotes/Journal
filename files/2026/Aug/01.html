Saturday 1st August

We started with a walk to the High Street to buy some things. I also went to the pharmacy to collect my tablets. We then spent some time reading the newspaper! I spent most of the rest of the morning doing the weekly and monthly accounts.

Then I tried to view the Adestra training video. It was noted that the video freezes after about 18 minutes. My attempt froze after five minutes (although it seemed to continue when I went back to it), so I downloaded it and ran it through Handbrake. Unfortunately that made no difference, but I had seen enough to try the new version.

The video didn't tell you how to find the new version, so I went to the old site and, as well as the password, I was asked for a code, which I got from the fob.

I am quite angry at what I found. We've waited for over a year for something that is less capable than the version it has replaced.

After lunch we had a FaceTime call with Paul and Victoria. Brandon, Victoria and the three children had just returned from the 'bird shop'.
