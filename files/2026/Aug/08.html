Saturday 8th August

A gentler day, our walk to the High Street to buy a newspaper being the only exertion.

Again it was warm and sunny, with no sign of rain. Angela pottered in the garden and I started investigating why my 'family history' web page is not showing the top level of our family tree properly. I fixed it later, although don't understand why what I did worked.

After lunch we spoke with Victoria. They were given a hotel room in Minneapolis due to missing their connection and so were a day late getting home. Brandon should have been back at work, but while they were flying home his company, Eighty Acres, went out of business. So, once again he is out of work and sending off applications. However, they are all over the country! It sounds as though they are trying to finish off the work on the house so that it is in the best state to be able to sell it.

Austin and Juniper go back to school on Tuesday.
