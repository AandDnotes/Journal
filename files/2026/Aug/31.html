31st August

The last day of meteorological summer and it was dry (after heavy rain over night) and reasonably warm, with some sunshine.

I paid for our tolls across the Dartford Crossing and got our carry-on cases down from the loft. I also made notes about our route on Thursday to Glemsford and Greyhound Cottage.

Angela walked to the High Street to buy some milk and then did some 'hoovering'.

Back on the BitBot, I stuck varying lengths of black electrical insulation tape on to paper and cut them out so as to make a track. They slid too much on the table top, so I tried a tea-towel. Same problem. Then I tried to coat the underside of each strip with glue, in the hope it would make them less slippy. It worked to some extent and the BitBot followed the track provided the angle between strips was substantially less than 90 degrees. Later I started looking at controlling the BitBot by tilting another micro:bit. I created a couple of test projects.
