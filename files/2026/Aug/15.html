Saturday 15th August

We took our usual walk to the High Street this morning. My walk was more leisurely than Angela's since she had to go back for her list. Even at that time, and with some high cloud, it was quite hot.

I spent a lot of the morning doing the weekly accounts and reconciling my spreadsheet against the bank statement. But it balanced, which is always a relief. After that I logged in to the Riviera web site to add our passport details, only to find that I had already done it. However, I did find that some of this month's cruises had already been cancelled due to the low water levels in the Danube! We will just need to keep our fingers crossed for October.

After lunch we had quite a long chat with Victoria. The children seemed to have survived their first week back at school (although it was only four days). Most of the discussion (monologue) was about Austin's teeth and the swimming pool repair options.
