Friday 28th August

I was up a little earlier than usual this morning since someone from Southern Water was due to fit a new water meter sometime between eight and midday. He actually arrived at about nine o'clock, just as Angela left to go to the High Street. It only took him ten minutes to remove the old meter and fit the new one.

I checked our bank account to see if my IET expenses had been paid in, but they hadn't. Then I noticed that it said it could take three days.

I had started writing my thought on political philosophy, but I listed all the things I could think of that a government could do for its citizens and created a link down to the appropriate section for each. I started writing notes on the first item in the list.

Later I sent Deshinder a copy of the IET's public liability insurance certificate that he need in order to book a lecture theatre at Brighton University. I also found and downloaded a set of guides on programming a BitBot XL.

My copy of Enjoying MG arrived and I found that my letter had been published. So I updated my 'publications' web pages.
