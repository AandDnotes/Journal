Wednesday 12th August

An even hotter day; our fifth heat wave! Angela went off walking with her friends, going down to Shoreham fort. A little later I drove to Ashington to join the Sussex Wanderers for coffee. Since I didn't have a navigator and, more importantly, an MG, I didn't do the run, but came home for a while. Angela and Felicity were walking down the road as I left for Maplehurst and the Wanderers barbecue. Apparently the run did not have any problems, but I encountered two sets of roadworks and one closed road!

It was hot under the gazebo, but the barbecue (and puddings) was good. I got home just before three o'clock.

This evening, between 6:10 and 7:13 the moon gradually covered the sun. A 92% eclipse. Angela had been given a pair of 'eclipse' glasses, so we were able to view the spectacle. The last eclipse was in 1990 and the next is in 2090, so it was worth trying to experience this one. It didn't go as dark as I'd expected and, although we saw the maximum coverage of the sun, it then went behind the house opposite.
